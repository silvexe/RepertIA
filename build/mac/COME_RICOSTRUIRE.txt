2.20 - Trascrizione degli audio: whisper.cpp v1.9.5 e FFmpeg minimo, compilati su un Mac Apple Silicon (Xcode CLT;
cmake da pip in un ambiente a parte). I modelli NON vanno nel pacchetto (si scaricano con il consenso dell'analista).
  whisper.cpp: sorgente https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/archive/refs/tags/v1.9.5.tar.gz
    (SHA-256 ff1a9053feb509ff9d7729703355541ae9690073a6b1c40eb692c962e0dc1720)
    cmake -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DBUILD_SHARED_LIBS=OFF -DGGML_METAL=ON -DGGML_METAL_EMBED_LIBRARY=ON \
      -DGGML_NATIVE=OFF -DCMAKE_OSX_ARCHITECTURES=arm64 -DCMAKE_OSX_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET=13.0 -DWHISPER_BUILD_TESTS=OFF \
      -DWHISPER_SDL2=OFF -DWHISPER_CURL=OFF
    cmake --build build --config Release --target whisper-cli -j 8
    -> build/bin/whisper-cli (solo librerie di sistema) in RepertIA.app/Contents/Resources/strumenti/whisper/bin/,
       con LICENSE (MIT) in strumenti/whisper/
  FFmpeg: sorgente ffmpeg-n9.0.2-36-g6882969ce9-sorgenti.tar.gz (lo stesso allegato alla Release)
    ./configure --arch=arm64 --cc=clang --extra-cflags=-mmacosx-version-min=13.0 --extra-ldflags=-mmacosx-version-min=13.0 \
      --disable-gpl --disable-nonfree --disable-shared --enable-static --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-ffplay \
      --disable-ffprobe --disable-network --disable-autodetect --enable-audiotoolbox --disable-everything \
      --enable-protocol=file,pipe \
      --enable-demuxer=ogg,mov,mp3,wav,aac,amr,flac,matroska,caf,aiff,asf,au,w64,mpegts,avi \
      --enable-decoder=opus,vorbis,aac,aac_latm,mp3,mp3float,mp2,amrnb,amrwb,flac,alac,pcm_s16le,pcm_s24le,pcm_s32le,\
pcm_f32le,pcm_u8,pcm_mulaw,pcm_alaw,pcm_s16be,pcm_s24be,adpcm_ima_qt,adpcm_ms,gsm,gsm_ms,wmav1,wmav2,h264,hevc,mpeg4,mjpeg,vp8,vp9,av1 \
      --enable-parser=opus,vorbis,aac,mpegaudio,flac,h264,hevc,mpeg4video,vp8,vp9,av1 --enable-muxer=wav,image2,mjpeg \
      --enable-encoder=pcm_s16le,mjpeg --enable-filter=aresample,aformat,anull,scale,format,null,select \
      --enable-swresample --enable-swscale
    make -j8  -> ffmpeg (licenza LGPL v2.1 o successiva) in strumenti/ffmpeg/bin/, con COPYING.LGPLv2.1 e LICENSE.md
